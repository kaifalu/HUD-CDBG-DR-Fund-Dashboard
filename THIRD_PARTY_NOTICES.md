Plotly.js 3.3.1 is bundled locally. Its license is in `assets/vendor/PLOTLY_LICENSE.txt`.

The single-file HTML builder uses the supplied Pako inflate library in `scripts/vendor/`; its original license header is retained.

## Geographic data

State, county/county-equivalent, and 2010 urban-area boundary assets derive from U.S. Census Bureau files. City/place coordinates and primary-county attributes derive from the supplied SimpleMaps U.S. Cities database. The application preserves its visible attribution. Original cities and shapefile inputs are not included in this deployment ZIP.
